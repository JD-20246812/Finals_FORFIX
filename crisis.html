Crisis Support

If you or someone near you is in danger, act now.

If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency room.

NCMH Crisis Hotline: 1553 (toll-free, Luzon)

NCMH Crisis Hotline (mobile): 0917-899-8727

Hopeline PH: 0917-558-4673

Please verify every hotline number and address with the official source before the website goes live.

What you can do right now

  • Stay with the person or ask someone to stay with you.
  • Move away from anything that could cause harm.
  • Call a hotline and say how you feel in your own words.
  • Try the breathing exercise while you wait.
